FitCircle iPhone layout test

Compare two temporary Home Screen apps. These pages contain synthetic cards only and do not read or change fitness records. Keep your real FitCircle installed.

  1. In Safari, open Standard status bar test, use Share → Add to Home Screen, then launch FC Standard.
  2. Check whether the cyan bottom line reaches the physical bottom, then tap the bottom control. Scroll to the last card, open/dismiss the keyboard, and rotate.
  3. Use Copy report and send the result with whether the bottom strip remains.
  4. For comparison, install and check Current status bar test as FC Control. It differs only in the status-bar metadata and app identity.

A screen/viewport height difference is expected when the status bar sits above the page. Compare physical bottom reach, not just that number.

If a link opens the regular fitness app instead, open FitCircle online, let its normal update finish, close and reopen Safari, then retry. Do not clear website data or remove your existing app.
